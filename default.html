Marketing

signals, displays, and processes used to advertise the value of something

by Charles Iliya Krempeaux

Marketing is the deployment of structured signals — whether honest, exaggerated, or deceptive — which are designed to affect and even manipulate the perceptions, choices, or behaviors of independent receivers to benefit the fitness, interests, or survival of the sender.
